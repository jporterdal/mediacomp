# Commit messages

- Do not add `Co-Authored-By` lines or any other AI attribution to commit messages.
- A commit body explains *why*, never *what*; the diff already shows what changed. Leave the body out when there is no why to add.
- OpenSpec artifact-only commits (`opsx change ...`, `Archive ...`, spec syncs) get a subject line only, with no body. The added, edited or moved artifacts already record what the commit does.
